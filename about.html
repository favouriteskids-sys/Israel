About EstateFlow

The platform built to help agents, landlords, and investors manage real estate with confidence.

Our Story

EstateFlow started with a simple observation: real estate professionals spend too much time juggling spreadsheets, sticky notes, and scattered tools. We built one platform that brings listings, deals, clients, and documents together in a single place.

Today, hundreds of agents and landlords across Africa trust EstateFlow to manage their portfolios — from first viewing to final signature.

Our Mission

We believe great tools should make complex work feel simple. Our mission is to give every real estate professional the technology to close more deals in less time, no matter how big or small their portfolio.
